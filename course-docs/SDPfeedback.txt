Software Development Plan [SDP]

INTRODUCTION/DELIVERABLES SECTION:
   Assessment: Excellent
      Strengths
         • All major project deliverables are identified and described.
         • Deliverables include documentation, prototypes, software
           components, reviews, testing activities, presentations, and
           final delivery artifacts.
         • Delivery dates and milestone timing are clearly specified.
         • Each deliverable includes sufficient detail regarding its
           purpose and expected content.
         • Deliverables align well with the overall project lifecycle.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Formal acceptance criteria are not explicitly defined.
         • Deliverable ownership is implied but not clearly assigned.
      Suggestions
         1. Add measurable acceptance criteria for each deliverable,
            including review and approval requirements.
         2. Designate a primary owner or lead reviewer for each major
            deliverable.

PROJECT RESOURCES SECTION:
   Assessment: Excellent
      Strengths
         • Hardware and software resources are thoroughly documented.
         • Hardware specifications are detailed and realistic.
         • Software resources include versions, purposes, and planned
           deployment technologies.
         • Development, testing, model training, and deployment
           environments are clearly described.
         • Resource descriptions demonstrate strong planning and
           technical understanding.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Machine-learning training resources are described at a high
           level rather than as specific hardware platforms.
         • Some deployment specifications remain tentative.
      Suggestions
         1. Identify the exact machine-learning training environment
            once model sizes and datasets are finalized.
         2. Finalize backend hosting specifications and document any
            contingency plans if resource requirements increase.

PROJECT ORGANIZATION SECTION:
   Assessment: Excellent
      Strengths
         • Responsibilities are clearly assigned to team members.
         • Ownership of major technical work areas is well defined.
         • Shared responsibilities are identified where appropriate.
         • Work assignments align logically with project activities.
         • The organization section provides excellent coverage of
           implementation, testing, documentation, and deployment.
      Areas for Improvement
         • No organizational chart is provided.
         • Communication and decision-making procedures are only
           implicitly described.
      Suggestions
         1. Add a simple organizational diagram showing reporting and
            coordination relationships among team members.
         2. Document procedures for conflict resolution, technical
            decision-making, and scope-change approval.

PROJECT SCHEDULE SECTION:
   Assessment: Excellent
      Strengths
         • Major milestones are clearly identified.
         • Dependencies and parallel activities are described.
         • A PERT chart is included.
         • Schedule activities cover the entire project lifecycle from
           requirements through final delivery.
         • Team meetings are scheduled and associated with specific
           project objectives.
         • The Task/Resource Table provides strong linkage between
           people, hardware, software, and project activities.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Critical-path activities are not explicitly identified.
         • Contingency time and schedule slack are not discussed.
      Suggestions
         1. Identify critical-path tasks and highlight activities that
            could delay final delivery if they slip.
         2. Include schedule contingency or buffer estimates for
            high-risk tasks such as model training and integration.
