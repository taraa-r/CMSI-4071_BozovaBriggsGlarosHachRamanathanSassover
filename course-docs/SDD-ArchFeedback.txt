COMMENTS:

The architectural SDD is exceptionally strong and represents one of the most
complete architectural design documents among senior capstone projects.  It
follows the prescribed architectural structure closely, maintains direct
traceability to the CSC and CSU decomposition in the requirements, and
consistently focuses on responsibilities, interfaces, quality attributes,
and major design decisions.

The strongest aspects are architectural rigor, requirements traceability,
explicit interface definitions, quality-attribute treatment, interaction
design, security considerations, performance-awareness, and excellent
architectural diagrams. Very few gaps were identified beyond some
implementation detail creep and a small number of architectural assumptions
that could be documented more explicitly.

The document demonstrates mature architectural design skills, outstanding
requirements traceability, strong quality-attribute awareness, thoughtful
interface definitions, and excellent component decomposition.  It is
unusually complete for a senior capstone project and closely follows the
expected SDD guidance.

The strongest sections are the System Objectives, Server CSC, Evaluation
CSC, Major Software Interactions, and Architectural Diagrams.  The primary
improvement opportunities are reducing a small amount of
implementation-level detail, strengthening discussion of scalability
assumptions, documenting a few additional architectural tradeoffs, and
explicitly identifying trust boundaries within the deployment architecture.

Overall Rating: Exceptional Architectural SDD
